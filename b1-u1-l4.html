Sharing the News

We often mix these two tenses when telling a story or sharing news. Here is the golden rule:

1. The News (Present Perfect)

Use the Present Perfect to announce a recent event or piece of news. The exact time is not important.
"I have bought a new car!" 🚗

2. The Details (Past Simple)

Once the news is introduced, switch to Past Simple to give specific details (when, where, who, how).
"I bought it yesterday from a friend." 📅

⚠️ Question Rule:
Questions asking for general experiences use Present Perfect: "Have you ever eaten sushi?"
Questions asking for specifics use Past Simple: "When did you eat it?"
