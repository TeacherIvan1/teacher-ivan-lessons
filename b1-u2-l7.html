Linking Past Events

To make our stories flow naturally, we use time connectors to show exactly when things happened in relation to each other.

The Big Four:

✔️ UNTIL: Up to a certain point in time. (I waited until it stopped raining.)
✔️ AS SOON AS: Immediately after something else. (As soon as he arrived, we started.)
✔️ MEANWHILE: At the same time, usually in a different place. (She cooked; meanwhile, I worked.)
✔️ EVENTUALLY: In the end, especially after a long delay or problem. (We got lost, but eventually found it.)
