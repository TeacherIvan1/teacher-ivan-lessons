Irregular Verbs: Storytelling

Irregular verbs do not end in -ed. You have to memorize their special forms to tell stories about the past! Here are the Top 8 most common irregular verbs:

1. Go ➔ went
5. Take ➔ took
2. Have ➔ had
6. Buy ➔ bought
3. See ➔ saw
7. Do ➔ did
4. Eat ➔ ate
8. Make ➔ made
⚠️ Careful! Negative sentences:
Use didn't + Base Verb. (Example: I didn't go to the park. NOT didn't went).
