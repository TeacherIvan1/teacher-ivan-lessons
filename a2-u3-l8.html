Why do you do it?

When we want to express the reason or purpose behind an action, we use the Infinitive of Purpose.

Structure:

Action + to + Base Verb

✔️ I study English to travel around the world.
✔️ He went to the gym to lift weights.

⚠️ Common Mistakes:
In Spanish, we say "para viajar". But in English, DO NOT use "for" + verb in this case!
❌ I study English for travel. (Incorrect)
❌ I study English for to travel. (Incorrect)
❌ I study English to travelling. (Incorrect)
