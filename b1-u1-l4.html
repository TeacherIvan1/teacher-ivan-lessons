Expressing Duration

We use the Present Perfect with For and Since to say how long an action has continued up to the present moment.

1. FOR (Period of Time)

We use FOR to talk about an amount or period of time.
"I have rested for three days." (1 day + 2 days + 3 days)

2. SINCE (Point in Time)

We use SINCE to talk about a specific starting point in the past.
"I have rested since Monday." (It started on Monday)

⚠️ Common Mistake:
Do not use "since" with an amount of time.
❌ I have worked here since 5 years.
✔️ I have worked here for 5 years.
