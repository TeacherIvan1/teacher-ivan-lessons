The Present Perfect Tense

Welcome to B1! We use the Present Perfect to talk about life experiences when the exact time is not important. It's about what you have done in your life up to now.

The Structure:

✔️ Subject + have/has + Past Participle (V3)
I have traveled to Europe. / He has built an app.

Ever and Never:

✔️ Ever: Used in questions (Have you ever...?). Meaning: "alguna vez".
✔️ Never: Used in affirmative sentences to give a negative meaning. Meaning: "nunca".

⚠️ Don't confuse Past Simple with Past Participle:
You must use the 3rd column of the irregular verbs!
❌ I have went to China. ➔ ✔️ I have gone to China.
