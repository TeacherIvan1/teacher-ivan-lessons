Life in the Past vs. Present

When we want to compare how things used to be, we combine the past tense of "To Be" (was / were) with Comparative Adjectives.

1. Was or Were?

Use Was for singular (I, He, She, It). Use Were for plural (You, We, They).
Life was simpler. / Cities were smaller.

2. Comparatives

Short words add -er (older, faster). Long words use more (more expensive).
Traveling was slower. / Cars were more expensive.

⚠️ Irregular Adjectives:
Good ➔ Better
Bad ➔ Worse
Far ➔ Farther
