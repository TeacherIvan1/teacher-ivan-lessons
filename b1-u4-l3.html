Historical Facts & Past Events

We use the Past Simple Passive to talk about history, inventions, and discoveries where the creation is more important than the creator.

Structure:

Object + was / were + Past Participle (V3)

Active: Pedro de Anzúrez founded Sucre in 1538.
Passive: Sucre was founded (by Pedro de Anzúrez) in 1538.

💡 Remember:
Use was for singular objects (The school was built).
Use were for plural objects (The pyramids were constructed).
