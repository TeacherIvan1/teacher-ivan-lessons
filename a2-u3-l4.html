The Power of "Will"

We use Going to for plans we made before. But we use Will or Won't for three specific situations:

1. Sudden Decisions (Decisiones rápidas)

When you decide to do something exactly in the moment you speak.
"Oh, it's raining! I will take an umbrella."

2. Promises (Promesas)

When you give your word to someone.
"I promise I won't tell your secret."

3. Predictions (Predicciones sin evidencia)

When you guess what will happen in the future (often used with I think).
"I think robots will build our houses in 2050."

⚠️ Grammar Rule:
Always use the Base Verb after "will" or "won't". Never use "to"!
❌ I will to call you.
✔️ I will call you. / I'll call you.
