The Past of the Past

We use the Past Perfect to describe an action that happened before another action in the past. We use the Past Simple for the action that happened second.

The Formula:

✔️ Subject + had + Past Participle (V3)
"When I arrived at the station, the train had left."
(Action 1: The train left. Action 2: I arrived.)

Time Markers:

We often use words like already, just, never, before, after, and by the time to connect the two actions.
"By the time I woke up, my mom had already made breakfast."

⚠️ Don't confuse HAVE with HAD:
Present Perfect (Have/Has) connects to the Present.
Past Perfect (Had) connects two actions in the Past.
