Your Final Mission

It's time to show what you know! Your final project is to record a short video or audio telling a story about your best holiday. Use this worksheet to write your script.

🎯 Grammar Checklist

• Past Simple (Regular & Irregular verbs)
• Verb To Be (Was / Were)
• Time Expressions (Last year, ago...)

🗣️ Recording Tips

• Speak loud and clear!
• Remember the -ed pronunciation (/t/, /d/, /id/).
• Smile while you speak!

1 Brainstorming (Wh- Questions)

Answer these questions with short sentences. This will give you the ideas for your story.

Where did you go and When did you travel?
Who did you go with?
How was the weather and the place? (Use adjectives)
What did you do there? (Name 2 or 3 activities)

2 Write your Script

Now, connect your ideas from Step 1 to write your full story. Remember to use Sequencing Words (First, Next, Then, After that, Finally).

Introduction: (Say hello, say where and when you went, and who was with you)
Body: (Tell the story. Use sequencing words and irregular verbs. Explain what you did and didn't do)
Conclusion: (Give your final opinion using travel adjectives)
