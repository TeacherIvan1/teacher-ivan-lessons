Omitting the Agent

When we describe scientific processes, environmental actions, or industrial manufacturing, we usually omit the agent (the person doing the action).

When do we omit the agent?

We do not use "by + person" when the agent is:

  • Unknown: "My bicycle was stolen." (I don't know who did it).
  • Obvious: "The thief was arrested." (Obviously, by the police).
  • Unimportant / General: "Paper is recycled here." (By people in general, it doesn't matter who).
💡 Pro Tip:
If the active sentence starts with words like Someone, People, They, or We, you should always omit the agent in the passive voice.
