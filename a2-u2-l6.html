The Golden Rule of Sports

In English, we don't just "practice" sports. Depending on the type of activity, we must choose between Play, Go, or Do.

Play

Sports with a BALL or competitive games.
  • ⚽ football
  • 🎾 tennis
  • 🎮 video games
  • ♟️ chess

Go

Activities ending in -ING.
  • 🏃 running
  • 🏊 swimming
  • 🚴 cycling
  • 🎣 fishing

Do

Martial arts, gymnastics, and individual routines.
  • 🥋 karate
  • 🧘 yoga
  • 🤸 gymnastics
  • 🏋️ CrossFit
