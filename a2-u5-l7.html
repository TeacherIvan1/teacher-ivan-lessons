Don't have to vs Mustn't

Level A2 | Unit 5 - Lesson 7 | Optional vs Prohibited

🏆 Score Challenge: 51 Points
⚖️

The Big Difference

Many students confuse these two negative forms, but they have completely different meanings!

Mustn't (100% Prohibited)

It means DO NOT DO IT. It is a strict rule or it is dangerous.
Example: You mustn't cheat on the exam.

Don't have to (0% Obligation)

It means IT IS NOT NECESSARY. It is optional. You can do it if you want, but you don't need to.
Example: Tomorrow is Sunday. You don't have to wake up early.

⚠️ Remember the 3rd Person:
For He, She, and It, we use doesn't have to (not "don't have to" and not "doesn't has to").
