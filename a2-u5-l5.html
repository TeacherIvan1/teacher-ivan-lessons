Obligations: Have to / Has to

We use have to when an action is necessary because it is a rule, a law, or a daily obligation.
We use don't have to to say that something is NOT necessary (it's optional).

The Rules:

✔️ I / You / We / They: have to / don't have to
✔️ He / She / It: has to / doesn't have to

Example: I have to work on Monday, but I don't have to work on Sunday.

⚠️ CAREFUL WITH QUESTIONS & NEGATIVES:
When using doesn't or does, the verb goes back to normal (have to).
❌ She doesn't has to go. ➔ ✔️ She doesn't have to go.
❌ Does he has to study? ➔ ✔️ Does he have to study?
