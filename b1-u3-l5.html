Formal Conditions

When setting strict rules, policies, or academic requirements, we often use formal conditional connectors instead of "If".

UNLESS (If... not)

Used to give a negative condition. It means an action will happen except if something else stops it.
Example: You cannot enter the school laboratory unless you wear safety glasses.

PROVIDED THAT (Only if / On the condition that)

Very formal. Used to emphasize that an action will happen only if a specific requirement is met.
Example: You may join the SAGMUN debate team provided that your grades are excellent.
