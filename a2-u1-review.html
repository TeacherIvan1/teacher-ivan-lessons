Memory Check!

Before you begin the final challenge of Unit 1, remember the core rules of the Past Simple:

To Be (Was / Were)

(+) I was / They were
(-) He wasn't / We weren't
(?) Was she...? / Where were you?

Action Verbs (Did)

(+) Regular (+ed) / Irregular (went, saw)
(-) didn't + base verb (didn't play)
(?) Did + subject + base verb?
