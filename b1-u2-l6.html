Narrating History

To talk about historical events, we must master Narrative Tenses and Time Prepositions.

Time Prepositions:

✔️ IN: Years, months, centuries (in 1825, in August, in the 19th century).
✔️ ON: Specific dates and days (on August 6th, on Monday).
✔️ AT: Precise times (at midnight, at 3:00 PM).
✔️ DURING: A period of time (during the Chaco War).
✔️ BY: Not later than a specific time (by 1935, they had finished).

⚠️ Don't forget the Tenses!
Combine these prepositions with Past Simple (facts), Past Continuous (background), and Past Perfect (events before other past events).
