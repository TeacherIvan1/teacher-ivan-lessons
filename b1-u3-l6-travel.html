Did you travel last year?

Level B1 | Unit 3 - Lesson 6 | Past Simple Questions

✈️

Asking about Past Experiences

To ask questions in the Past Simple (for all verbs except To Be), we must use the auxiliary verb DID.

Yes/No Questions

Did + Subject + Base Verb + Complement?
Example: Did you book the flight tickets?

Information Questions (Wh-)

Wh- Word + did + Subject + Base Verb?
Example: Where did they stay in Europe?

⚠️ THE GOLDEN RULE:
When you use the auxiliary Did, the main verb never changes to the past form!
✔️ Did she travel abroad?
❌ Did she travelled abroad?
