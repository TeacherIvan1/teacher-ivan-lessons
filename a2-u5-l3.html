Strict Rules: Must & Mustn't

We use Must to talk about strong obligations (100% necessary). We use Mustn't (Must not) to talk about strict prohibitions (100% illegal or banned).

Structure:

✔️ Subject + must + Base Verb
We must wear the school uniform. (Rule)

✔️ Subject + mustn't + Base Verb
You mustn't cheat on the exam. (Prohibition)

⚠️ THE "TO" TRAP CONTINUES:
Just like "should", NEVER use the word "to" after must.
❌ You must to pay attention.
❌ She musts arrive on time.
✔️ You must pay attention.
