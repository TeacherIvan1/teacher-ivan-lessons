Questions and Short Answers

To make a question in the Past Simple with the verb To Be, we simply invert the subject and the verb.

Yes / No Questions Short Answers (+) Short Answers (-)
Was the flight long? Yes, it was. No, it wasn't.
Were the beaches crowded? Yes, they were. No, they weren't.
🧳 Travel Adjectives to remember:
Awesome, breathtaking, comfortable, crowded, expensive, exhausting, relaxing, awful.
