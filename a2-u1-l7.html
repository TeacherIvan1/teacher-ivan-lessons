Asking about the Past

To ask questions in the Past Simple, we use the auxiliary verb Did for all subjects (I, you, he, she, it, we, they).

Yes/No Questions & Short Answers

Did + Subject + Base Verb ?
Q: Did you travel to Sucre?
A: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

Wh- Questions

Wh- Word + did + Subject + Base Verb ?
Q: Where did she go?
A: She went to the museum.

⚠️ THE GOLDEN RULE AGAIN:
When you use Did in a question, the main verb returns to its normal base form!
✔️ Did you play?
❌ Did you played?
