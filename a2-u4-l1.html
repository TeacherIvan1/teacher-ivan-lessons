Comparing Two Things

When comparing two places or objects with a short adjective (1 syllable), we add -ER to the adjective and use the word THAN.

Spelling Rules:

✔️ General rule: add -er (cold ➔ colder)
✔️ Ends in -e: add -r (nice ➔ nicer)
✔️ Ends in consonant-vowel-consonant: double the last letter (big ➔ bigger, hot ➔ hotter)
✔️ Ends in -y: change 'y' to 'i' and add -er (dry ➔ drier, heavy ➔ heavier)

⚠️ Irregular Adjectives:
Good ➔ Better
Bad ➔ Worse
Far ➔ Farther / Further
