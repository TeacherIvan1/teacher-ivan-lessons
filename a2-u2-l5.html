How do you do it?

We use Adverbs of Manner to describe HOW an action is performed. They usually go at the end of the sentence or directly after the verb.

Rule Adjective (Describes nouns) Adverb (Describes verbs)
General: add -ly safe / quick safely / quickly
Ends in 'y': -ily heavy / easy heavily / easily
Irregular good
fast
hard		 well
fast (Not fastly)
hard (Not hardly)
⚠️ Adjective vs. Adverb:
He is a safe lifter. (Describes the person/noun)
He lifts the kettlebell safely. (Describes the action/verb)

Change the adjectives in brackets into Adverbs of Manner to complete the sentences.
