Linking Past Events: So vs Because

When telling a story in the past, we use linking words to connect our ideas and show cause and effect.

BECAUSE (Reason)

It answers the question "Why?". It introduces the cause.
El Michi went to the vet because he was sick.

SO (Result)

It introduces the effect or consequence of an action.
El Michi was sick, so he went to the vet.

⚠️ Don't mix them up!
Read the sentence carefully. Is the second part the REASON for the first part, or is it the RESULT?
