Hypothetical Leadership & Advice

When we want to give advice or imagine ourselves in a position of power (like a boss, a president, or a Headmaster), we use the Second Conditional.

The "WERE" Rule

In hypothetical situations, we use were instead of "was" for ALL subjects (I, he, she, it).

If I were you, I would [verb]... ➔ Used to give strong advice.
If she were the principal, she would [verb]... ➔ Used to imagine a different reality.

💡 Pro Tip: Do not use "to" after would.
✔️ I would change the rules.
❌ I would to change the rules.
