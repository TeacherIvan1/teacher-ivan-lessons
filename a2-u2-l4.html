Neutral Feelings & Strong Dislikes

When an activity is "okay" for us (we don't love it, but we don't hate it), we use don't mind. When we absolutely dislike an activity, we use hate or can't stand.

Neutral (It's OK)

I / You / We / They ➔ don't mind + ING
He / She / It ➔ doesn't mind + ING

Example: I don't mind waking up early.

Strong Dislike (I really dislike it)

I / You / We / They ➔ hate / can't stand + ING
He / She / It ➔ hates / can't stand + ING

Example: She hates running in the rain.

⚠️ THE ING RULE:
After verbs of preference (like, love, hate, don't mind, can't stand), the next verb MUST end in -ING. Never use "to"!
✔️ I don't mind lifting weights.
❌ I don't mind to lift weights.
