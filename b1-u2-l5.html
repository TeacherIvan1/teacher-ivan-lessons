The Art of Storytelling

To tell a dramatic story or describe a movie scene, we must combine the three narrative tenses correctly.

1. Past Continuous (The Scene)

Sets the background atmosphere.
It was raining heavily. Tommy was smoking a cigarette.

2. Past Perfect (The Backstory)

Explains what happened before the scene started.
Before he arrived, the police had searched the pub.

3. Past Simple (The Action)

Describes the main sequence of events or interruptions.
Suddenly, the door opened and a man walked in.
