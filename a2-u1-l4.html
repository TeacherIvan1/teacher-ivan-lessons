Regular Verbs: Spelling & Pronunciation

Most verbs in English are regular. We form the Past Simple by adding -ed to the verb. However, there are some spelling rules and three different ways to pronounce the ending!

Rule Present Past (-ed)
General (+ed) walk walked
Ends in 'e' (+d) arrive arrived
Consonant + y (y ➔ ied) study studied
CVC (Double consonant + ed) stop stopped

/ id /

After T or D sounds
want➔wanted, need➔needed

/ t /

After unvoiced sounds (p, k, s, ch, sh, f)
watch➔watched, work➔worked

/ d /

After voiced sounds (l, n, r, v, vowels)
play➔played, live➔lived
