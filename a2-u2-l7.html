Direct Instructions (The Imperative)

When a coach gives commands to avoid injuries, they use the Imperative. The imperative is very direct: we DO NOT use the subject "You".

Positive Commands

Start directly with the Base Verb.
Example: Bend your knees. / Keep your back straight.

Negative Commands

Start with Don't + Base Verb.
Example: Don't drop the barbell! / Don't look down.

⚠️ Common Mistake:
Never use "Not" alone for negative commands. Always use Don't!
❌ Not bend your back. ➔ ✔️ Don't bend your back.
