The Negative Past Simple

To talk about things you did not do in the past, we use the auxiliary verb didn't (did not).

Structure:

Subject + didn't + Base Verb

Example: We didn't stay at home.

⚠️ THE GOLDEN RULE:
When you use didn't, the main verb returns to its normal base form! Do not use "-ed" or irregular past forms.
✔️ I didn't play.
❌ I didn't played.
