More expensive or cheaper?

Objective: Compare prices and objects using short and long comparative adjectives.

1. Vocabulary: Shopping & Money

Learn these words to talk about prices when you go shopping.

💸 Expensive Caro / Costoso
🏷️ Cheap Barato / Económico
🛒 Buy Comprar
💳 Pay Pagar

2. Grammar: Short vs Long Comparatives

When we compare two things, we must check the size of the adjective. The rule for "cheap" (short) is different from the rule for "expensive" (long).

Short Adjective (Cheap)
Adjective + ER + than

Add -er to the end of short words.

"Water is cheaper than soda."
"A jump rope is cheaper than a barbell."
Long Adjective (Expensive)
More + Adjective + than

Do NOT add -er. Put "more" before the word.

"A car is more expensive than a bike."
"Nike shoes are more expensive than sandals."
🚨 THE DOUBLE COMPARATIVE TRAP

You must choose ONE rule. Never put "more" and "-er" together! That creates a double comparative, and it sounds terrible to native speakers.

❌ This book is more cheaper than that one.
❌ This watch is expensiver.
✅ This book is CHEAPER. / This watch is MORE EXPENSIVE.

Reading Context: Shopping for CrossFit

"At Colegio Fielding, we teach students the value of money. When Lau and I go shopping for CrossFit equipment in Sucre, we always compare prices. For example, a professional barbell is more expensive than a simple jump rope. However, good running shoes are very important. My Nike Pegasus Trail 5s are more expensive than normal sneakers, but they protect my knees!"

"Yesterday, Lau bought a new jump rope because it was cheaper than buying new weights. We always try to save money when we pay for our gear."

"But there is one big financial problem in our house. Tuna is becoming more expensive every day, and El Michi eats a lot of it! I told him: 'You need to find a job to pay for your food!' He just closed his eyes. For him, sleeping on the sofa is cheaper than paying rent. In fact, it is completely free!"
