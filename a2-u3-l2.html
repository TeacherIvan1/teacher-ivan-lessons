✈️

Asking about Future Plans

To ask Yes/No questions about someone's plans, we swap the verb To Be and the Subject.

Questions

Am / Is / Are + Subject + going to + Base Verb?

Example: Are you going to travel to La Paz?
Example: Is she going to buy a ticket?

Short Answers

We answer using the verb To Be. We DO NOT repeat "going to".
✔️ Yes, I am. / Yes, she is. / Yes, they are.
✔️ No, I'm not. / No, she isn't. / No, they aren't.

⚠️ Don't contract positive short answers!
❌ Yes, I'm. ➔ ✔️ Yes, I am.
❌ Yes, she's. ➔ ✔️ Yes, she is.
