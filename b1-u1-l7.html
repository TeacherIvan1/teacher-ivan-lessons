Mind your Modals

Mental health is very important. To give advice or state rules about well-being, we use Modal Verbs. They show the "attitude" of the speaker.

1. Advice (Good Ideas)

Should / Shouldn't: Used to give recommendations or say what is a good/bad idea.
"You are stressed. You should relax."

2. Obligation vs No Obligation

Have to / Must: 100% necessary. A strict rule.
Don't have to: 0% obligation. It is completely optional.
Mustn't: 100% Prohibited. Do not do it!

⚠️ Grammar Alert:
Never use "to" after should, must, or mustn't.
❌ You should to sleep. ➔ ✔️ You should sleep.
However, have to and don't have to ALWAYS include "to".
