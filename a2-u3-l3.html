Expressing Determination

We use the negative form of "Be going to" to express a firm decision NOT to do something. It is perfect for talking about discipline, avoiding bad habits, or refusing to give up.

The Negative Structure

Subject + am not / isn't / aren't + going to + Base Verb

  • I am not ('m not) going to quit.
  • He/She/It is not (isn't) going to lose.
  • You/We/They are not (aren't) going to fail.
⚠️ Don't use "Don't / Doesn't":
❌ She doesn't going to give up.
✔️ She isn't going to give up.
