The Exceptions to the Rule

Not all adjectives use "-er" or "the -est". The three most common adjectives in English are irregular. You must memorize their Comparative and Superlative forms.

The Big Three:

1. Good ➔ Better (than) ➔ (The) Best

2. Bad ➔ Worse (than) ➔ (The) Worst

3. Far ➔ Farther (than) ➔ (The) Farthest

⚠️ Don't invent words!
❌ "Gooder", "More good", "Baddest", "Badder", "Most far" ➔ Do NOT exist in English!
