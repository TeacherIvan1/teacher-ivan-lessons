📘 Quick Review: Present Simple

We use the Present Simple to talk about habits, routines, and permanent situations. Remember to add -s or -es for He, She, It.

🏃‍♂️
I / You / We / They
"I usually wake up at 5:30 am."
🏋️‍♀️
He / She / It
"He does CrossFit in the evening."
