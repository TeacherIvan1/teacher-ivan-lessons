As healthy as...

Objective: Compare health habits and lifestyles using "as...as" correctly.

1. Vocabulary: Health & Lifestyle

Learn these adjectives to describe how good or bad a habit is for your body.

🥗 Healthy Saludable / Sano
🍔 Unhealthy Poco saludable
🏃‍♂️ Active Activo
💪 Fit En buena forma

2. Grammar: Lifestyle Comparisons

We use as [adjective] as to compare two habits, foods, or people that are at the same level. If they are different, we use not as [adjective] as.

Healthy Equivalents (+)
as + ADJECTIVE + as
"Apples are as healthy as oranges."
"Lau is as fit as a professional athlete."
Unhealthy Differences (-)
NOT as + ADJECTIVE + as
"Burgers are not as healthy as salads."
"Watching TV is not as active as running."
🚨 THE "AS... LIKE" TRAP

In Spanish, you say "Él es tan fuerte como un toro". Because "como" often translates to "like", students make a huge mistake and say "as strong like a bull". In comparisons of equality, you must always use AS twice!

❌ Soda is not as healthy like water.
❌ He is as fit how me.
✅ Soda is not AS healthy AS water.

Reading Context: Fitness and Fast Food

"At Colegio Fielding, I always tell my students that eating well is very important. A burger is delicious, but it is not as healthy as a good salad. If you want to have energy for your History classes, you need to eat well!"

"In my personal life, fitness is essential. My partner Lau trains every day at our box in Sucre. She is as fit as a professional athlete! We do CrossFit together, and she is as strong as me. We also drink a lot of water because soda is not as good as water for your body."

"And then there is El Michi. Is he as active as us? No, he is definitely not as active as a normal cat. He eats tuna and sleeps on his favorite sofa. His lifestyle is not as healthy as ours, but he is very happy!"
