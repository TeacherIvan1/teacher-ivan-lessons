When did it happen?

We use time expressions at the beginning or end of a sentence to specify exactly when a past action occurred.

LAST (el/la pasado/a) YESTERDAY (ayer) AGO (hace...)
last night
last week
last month
last year
last Tuesday		 yesterday morning
yesterday afternoon
yesterday evening
(Not: yesterday night ➔ last night)		 10 minutes ago
two hours ago
a few days ago
five years ago
📌 Prepositions of Time: IN vs ON
Use IN for years and months: in 2024, in May.
Use ON for days and specific dates: on Friday, on October 12th.
