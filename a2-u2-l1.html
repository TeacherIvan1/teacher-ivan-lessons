I love running

Objective: Express hobbies, likes, and dislikes using verbs of preference + ING.

1. Vocabulary: Hobbies & Activities

Learn these action verbs to talk about the activities you do in your free time.

🏃‍♂️ Run Correr Activity: Running
🌅 Wake up Despertar Activity: Waking up
👟 Research Investigar (en internet) Activity: Researching
🛋️ Sleep Dormir Activity: Sleeping

2. Grammar: Like / Love / Hate + ING

When you talk about activities you like or dislike in general, you must add -ING to the action verb. It turns the action into a "thing" (a noun).

The Rule
Subject + Love / Like / Hate / Enjoy + Verb-ING

Use this to talk about your hobbies and daily preferences.

"I love reading books." (Amo leer libros).
"She enjoys playing games." (Ella disfruta jugar).
🚨 THE SPANISH TRAP

In Spanish, we use the base verb: "Me gusta correr". Do NOT say "I like run" in English. This is a very common A1 mistake. In A2, you must say "I like running".

❌ I love wake up early.
✅ I love waking up early.

Note: You can also say "I like TO run", but using -ING is much more natural when talking about general hobbies, especially after the verb "Enjoy".

Reading Context: The 5:30 AM Routine

"I have a very energetic morning routine. I love waking up early. Every day, I enjoy running at 5:30 AM before my classes at Colegio Fielding. It helps me prepare for the day!"

"Because I run so much, I like researching high-performance shoes. I love wearing my Brooks Hyperion Elite 4 on the track. However, I hate running in bad weather without my Nike Pegasus Trail 5 Gore-Tex."

"At home, things are very different. Perlita the cat absolutely hates exercising. She enjoys sleeping on the sofa all day and watching me come back tired. We definitely have different hobbies!"
