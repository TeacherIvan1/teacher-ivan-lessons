Consistency & Frequency

We use Adverbs of Frequency to describe how often we do an activity or a routine.

100% Always 80% Usually 50% Sometimes 10% Rarely 0% Never
⚠️ THE POSITION RULE:
1. Before Main Verbs: Subject + Adverb + Verb.
✔️ I always train at 6 AM. (❌ I train always)

2. AFTER the verb To Be: Subject + Am/Is/Are + Adverb.
✔️ He is never late to the gym. (❌ He never is late)
