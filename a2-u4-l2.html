Short vs. Long Adjectives

We already know that for short adjectives we add -ER (cheap ➔ cheaper). But what happens with long words?

Long Adjectives (2 or more syllables)

DO NOT add "-er". Instead, put the word MORE before the adjective.
✔️ Expensive ➔ more expensive (than)
✔️ Beautiful ➔ more beautiful (than)
✔️ Comfortable ➔ more comfortable (than)

⚠️ Fatal Error:
Never use "more" and "-er" together!
❌ more cheaper ➔ ✔️ cheaper
❌ expensiver ➔ ✔️ more expensive
