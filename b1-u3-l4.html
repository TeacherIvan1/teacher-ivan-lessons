The Third Conditional

We use the Third Conditional to talk about impossible past situations. It is often used to express regrets or to explain what went wrong (like finding a bug in your code after launching an app!).

Structure:

If + Past Perfect [had + V3],
would/wouldn't have + Past Participle [V3]

Example: If I had saved my HTML file, I wouldn't have lost my project.
