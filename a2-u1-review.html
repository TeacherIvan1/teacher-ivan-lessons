The Past Simple Tense

Welcome to the final challenge of Unit 1! To pass, you must remember the rules of the past.

The Summary:

✔️ Affirmative: Subject + Verb in Past (Regular -ed or Irregular).
✔️ Negative: Subject + didn't + Base Verb.
✔️ Questions: Did + Subject + Base Verb + ?

⚠️ THE DID TRAP:
When you use did or didn't, the action verb NEVER goes in the past!
❌ I didn't went. ➔ ✔️ I didn't go.
❌ Did she played? ➔ ✔️ Did she play?
