When does it happen?

To talk about schedules and routines correctly, we need the Prepositions of Time. Think of them like a triangle: from general (IN) to very specific (AT).

IN

Months, Years & Parts of the day
  • in the morning
  • in the afternoon
  • in May
  • in 2026

ON

Days of the week & Specific Dates
  • on Monday
  • on weekends
  • on April 16th
  • on my birthday

AT

Exact Times & "Night"
  • at 5:30 AM
  • at 8 o'clock
  • at night
  • at midnight
⚠️ The Exception Rule:
We say IN the morning, IN the afternoon, IN the evening... but we ALWAYS say AT night.
