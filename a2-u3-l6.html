Looking into the Crystal Ball

When we predict the future based on our feelings or beliefs (not solid evidence), we use Will / Won't. We often start these sentences with opinion phrases.

I think... (Creo que...)
I don't think... (No creo que...)
I hope... (Espero que...)
I'm sure... (Estoy seguro de que...)

Examples:

✔️ I think the new video game will be amazing.
✔️ I'm sure they won't lose the tournament.

⚠️ The "I don't think" Rule:
In English, it is much more natural to make the opinion negative, rather than the will.
❌ I think it won't rain. (Sounds weird)
✔️ I don't think it will rain. (Perfect!)
