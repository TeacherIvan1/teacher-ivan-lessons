Future Plans & Intentions

When we have a plan, a goal, or a firm intention for the future, we use the structure Be going to.

The Formula

(+) Subject + am/is/are + going to + Verb (Base)
I am going to study Medicine.

(-) Subject + am not/isn't/aren't + going to + Verb (Base)
She isn't going to fail the exam.

⚠️ Don't forget the verb To Be!
Many students say ❌ "I going to pass". That is incorrect!
You must say ✔️ "I am going to pass."
