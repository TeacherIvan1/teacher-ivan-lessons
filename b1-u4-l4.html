Present Perfect Passive

We use this structure to report recent actions or results where the "what" is more important than the "who". It's standard for update logs and status reports.

Structure:

Object + have / has + been + Past Participle (V3)

Active: The developers have fixed the bug.
Passive: The bug has been fixed.

*Remember: Use "has been" for singular and "have been" for plural objects.
