Going to vs. Will

Level A2 | Unit 3 - Lesson 7 | The Ultimate Future Test

⚖️

Choosing the Right Future

Both "Going to" and "Will" talk about the future, but they have completely different feelings. You must read the context before choosing!

📅 BE GOING TO (The Planner)

Use it for organized plans, firm intentions, and things you decided BEFORE speaking.
"I bought the tickets. I am going to travel to La Paz."

⚡ WILL (The Spontaneous)

Use it for sudden decisions made AT THE MOMENT of speaking, promises, or reactions.
"The phone is ringing! I will answer it."

A Read the Context

Look at the situations. Decide if it's a Plan (Going to) or a Sudden Decision (Will). Write the correct form with the verb in brackets.

1. (At a restaurant)
Waiter: Are you ready to order?
You: Yes, I (have) the chicken.
2. (A life plan)
I studied very hard. Next year, I (be) an engineer.
3. (A reaction)
A: Oh no! It is raining outside!
B: Don't worry. I (give) you my umbrella.
4. (An organized trip)
We (fly) to Miami tomorrow. Here are the tickets!

B Everyday Dialogues

Complete the conversations using Will or Be going to.

Ivan: Are you busy this weekend?
Santiago: Yes, I (study) for my math exam. I planned it yesterday.

Lau: That's okay! I (pay) for your coffee.
Coach: Why are you wearing those sports shoes?

C The Weekend Plan vs. The Surprise

Read the story. Fill in the gaps correctly. Is it a plan or a sudden reaction?

Tomorrow is Saturday. I (1. visit) my grandmother in the morning. We planned this last week! After that, my friends and I (2. play) football at the park.

Wait, my phone is ringing. I (3. answer) it.
