Going to vs. Will

Level A2 | Unit 3 - Lesson 7 | The Ultimate Future Test (31 Points)

⚖️

Choosing the Right Future

To master the future in English, you must read the context. Does the person have a plan, or did they decide right now?

📅 BE GOING TO (The Planner)

Use it for organized plans, firm intentions, and things you decided BEFORE speaking.
"I bought the tickets. I am going to travel to Sucre."

⚡ WILL (The Spontaneous)

Use it for sudden decisions made AT THE MOMENT of speaking, promises, or predictions without evidence.
"The phone is ringing! I will answer it."
