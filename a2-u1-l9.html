How to Tell a Good Story

When we tell a story about a trip or a historic event, we need to organize our ideas. We use Sequencing Words at the beginning of the sentence to show the order of events.

1. First, we arrived at the plaza. (Primero...)
2. Next, we watched the parade. (Luego/Siguiente...)
3. Then, we took a lot of photos. (Entonces/Después...)
4. After that, we ate delicious food. (Después de eso...)
5. Finally, we went back home. (Finalmente...)

*Note: "Next", "Then", and "After that" can be used in any order in the middle of the story. Always use a comma (,) after the sequencing word!
