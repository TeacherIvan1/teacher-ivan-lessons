The Passive Voice (Past Simple)

When we talk about history, inventions, or facts, we often use the Passive Voice. We use it when the *action* or the *object* is more important than "who" did it.

The Formula:

Object + was / were + Past Participle (V3)

Active: The Greeks invented the Olympics.
Passive: The Olympics were invented by the Greeks.

⚠️ Singular vs. Plural:
Use was for I/He/She/It (e.g., The stadium was built).
Use were for You/We/They (e.g., The medals were given).
