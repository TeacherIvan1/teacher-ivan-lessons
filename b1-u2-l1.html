Past Continuous vs Past Simple

Welcome to Unit 2! We often use these two tenses together to show that a longer action in the past was interrupted by a shorter action.

The Long Action (Past Continuous)

Uses was/were + verb(-ing). It sets the background scene.
I was running in the park...

The Interruption (Past Simple)

Uses the 2nd column (V2). It is the sudden event.
...when I fell.

⚠️ When vs While:
We usually use While before the Past Continuous (the long action).
We usually use When before the Past Simple (the short interruption).
