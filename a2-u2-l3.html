The Gym Dictionary

To understand a fitness routine or a CrossFit WOD (Workout of the Day), you need to know the correct names for the equipment.

🎱
Kettlebell
A heavy iron ball with a handle.
🪢
Jump Rope
Used for cardio and double-unders.
🏋️
Barbell
A long metal bar for heavy lifting.
🧱
Box
A wooden box used for jumping.
💪
Dumbbell
A short bar with weights for one hand.
🧗
Pull-up Bar
A high metal bar to lift your body.
