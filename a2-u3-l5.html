Asking for Favors & Making Offers

We already know that "Will" is used for the future, but we also use it in questions to ask someone to do something for us (a request) or to offer our help.

Requests (Pedir un favor)

Will + you + Base Verb + please?
"Will you open the window, please?"
"Will you help me with this box?"

Short Answers

When someone asks you for a favor using "Will", you answer with:
✔️ Yes, I will. / Sure! / Of course.
❌ No, I won't. / I'm sorry, I can't.

⚠️ Politeness Rule:
Using "Will you...?" is a polite way to give an order or ask for help. It sounds much better than saying "Open the window!".
