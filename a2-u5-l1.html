Health and Advice

When we want to give a recommendation, advice, or say what is a good idea, we use the modal verb Should or Shouldn't.

The Formula:

✔️ Subject + should + Base Verb
You should eat more vegetables.

✔️ Subject + shouldn't + Base Verb
He shouldn't drink soda every day.

⚠️ THE "TO" TRAP:
Modal verbs NEVER use the word "to" after them, and they NEVER take an "s" for he/she/it.
❌ You should to eat apples. (Wrong!)
❌ She shoulds sleep more. (Wrong!)
✔️ You should eat apples.
