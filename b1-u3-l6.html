Predicting the Future

When we talk about the future of technology and education, we use different structures depending on our certainty and the type of action.

1. Will vs. Might (Predictions & Possibilities)

Use Will / Won't when you are certain about a prediction.
Use Might / Might not when something is possible, but you are not 100% sure.

2. Future Continuous (Actions in Progress)

Use this structure to describe an action that will be in progress at a specific time in the future.
Structure: will be + verb(ing)
Example: This time next year, I will be studying history with VR glasses.
