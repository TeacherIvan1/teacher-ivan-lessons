Not Giving Up!

When we want to express determination, reject a bad plan, or answer questions about the future, we use the negative and interrogative forms of Be going to.

The Negative Form

Use 'm not / isn't / aren't + going to + verb.
We aren't going to quit! (¡No vamos a rendirnos!)
She isn't going to fail.

Questions & Short Answers

Q: Are you going to study today?
A: Yes, I am. / No, I 'm not.

Q: Is she going to travel?
A: Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

⚠️ Golden Rule for Short Answers:
Do NOT write "going to" in a short answer!
❌ Are you going to travel? Yes, I am going to. (WRONG)
✔️ Are you going to travel? Yes, I am. (CORRECT)
