In this B1 Project, you will document a healthy habit you have developed (e.g., doing CrossFit, drinking more water, using a fitness app). You must structure your story using the grammar from Unit 1.

Checklist - You must include:

✔️ Present Perfect: To talk about experiences ("I have been to...", "I have used...").
✔️ Past Perfect: For the "past of the past" ("Before I started, I had never...").
✔️ Modals: Advice or rules you learned ("You must stretch...", "You should...").
