Defining Relative Clauses

We use relative clauses to give essential information about a person, thing, or place. Without this information, the sentence would not make complete sense.

WHO / THAT ➔ For people.
The programmer who designed the app is a genius.

WHICH / THAT ➔ For things and animals.
I bought a smartwatch which tracks my calories.

WHERE ➔ For places.
This is the classroom where we study English.

WHOSE ➔ For possession (whose = de quien).
That is the student whose laptop is broken.
