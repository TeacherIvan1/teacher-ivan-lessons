Being Number One

When we compare three or more things to say which one is the "extreme" or the "highest degree" in a group, we use Superlative Adjectives.

The Rules:

✔️ Short Adjectives: THE + Adjective + -EST
(tall ➔ the tallest | hot ➔ the hottest | heavy ➔ the heaviest)

✔️ Long Adjectives: THE + MOST + Adjective
(expensive ➔ the most expensive | intelligent ➔ the most intelligent)

⚠️ Irregular Superlatives:
Good ➔ The best
Bad ➔ The worst
Far ➔ The farthest / The furthest
Always remember to include the word "THE"!
