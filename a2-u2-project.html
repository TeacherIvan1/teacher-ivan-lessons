Your Grammar Arsenal

To design an excellent weekly routine, you need to use the tools from Unit 2. Keep these rules in mind:

1. Time & Frequency:
always / usually / never
in the morning, on Monday, at 6 PM
2. Play, Go, Do:
play football, go running, do CrossFit
3. Preferences + ING:
I love lifting / I hate waiting
4. Safety (Should/Must):
You should stretch. / You mustn't drop the weights!

A Routine Warm-up

Before writing your project, test your memory. Complete the sentences correctly.

1. I usually (play/go/do) CrossFit 6:00 AM.
2. My gym partner loves (lift) heavy (equipment: b... / k...).
3. We never (play/go/do) tennis Sundays.
4. To protect your body, you (advice) keep your (espalda) straight.

B The Plan Guide

Follow these 4 steps to structure your Workout Plan. Read the examples to get inspired.

Step 1: Your Preferences
What activities do you like or hate?
Example: "I am very active. I love running, but I hate swimming."
Step 2: Schedule & Frequency
When do you train? Use adverbs of frequency and prepositions.
Example: "I always go to the gym on Mondays and Wednesdays at 5:30 AM."
Step 3: The WOD (Workout of the Day)
What equipment do you use? (kettlebells, jump rope, boxes).
Example: "In the gym, I usually do CrossFit. I lift heavy kettlebells and use the jump rope."
Step 4: A Safety Tip
Give one tip using 'should', 'must', or an Imperative.
Example: "To prevent injuries, you should stretch. Don't bend your back!"
