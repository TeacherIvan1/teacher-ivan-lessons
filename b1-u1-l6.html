Been or Gone?

Both are past participles of the verb "go", but they have very different meanings in the Present Perfect tense.

BEEN (Went and came back)

Use been to say that someone visited a place, but they are no longer there (they returned). It is a life experience.
"I have been to Paris three times." (I am not in Paris now).

GONE (Went and is still there)

Use gone to say that someone traveled to a place and has not returned yet.
"Where is Leonardo? He has gone to China." (He is in China right now).

⚠️ Preposition Rule:
With "been" and "gone", we always use the preposition to, never "in".
❌ I have been in Miami. ➔ ✔️ I have been to Miami.
